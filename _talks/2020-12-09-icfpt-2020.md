Presentation of our ICFPT 2020 paper introducing a framework that alters the
computational structure of multiple LSTM models so their memory requirements can
be co-optimised for a target FPGA, reaching 3× to 5× improved performance over
the state of the art at equal accuracy loss.

Joint work with Pedro Trancoso, Ioannis Sourdis and Christos-Savvas Bouganis.
