Source code for the PROTAC degradation activity predictor described in
*Artificial Intelligence in the Life Sciences* (2024): a curated dataset built
from PROTAC-DB and PROTAC-Pedia, and a model combining protein, cell-line and
molecular fingerprint embeddings to predict degradation activity.

See the [full write-up](/publication/2024-08-24-Modeling-PROTAC-degradation-activity-with-machine-learning)
for the paper, evaluation and citation.
