Source code for PROTAC-Splitter (*Journal of Cheminformatics*, 2026): a
Transformer model and a graph-based XGBoost model for annotating PROTAC
substructures, combined in an adaptive strategy, together with the synthetic
corpus of ~1.3M annotated PROTACs used to train them.

See the [full write-up](/publication/2026-02-20-PROTAC-Splitter) for the paper,
approach and citation.
