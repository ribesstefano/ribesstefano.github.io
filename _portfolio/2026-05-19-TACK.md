Code and data release accompanying the TACK paper (KDD 2026): aggregation and
harmonisation of three public PROTAC repositories into a single dataset, plus the
statistical benchmarking pipeline used to compare degradation activity models
(XGBoost, MLP, PROTAC-STAN) with proper scaffold-based cross-validation and
significance testing.

See the [full write-up](/publication/2026-05-19-TACK) for the paper, findings and
citation.
